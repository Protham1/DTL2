Ludwig Boltzmann and Statistical Mechanics

Overview

Ludwig Boltzmann, an Austrian physicist, is renowned for his development of statistical mechanics and the kinetic theory of gases. His work bridged macroscopic thermodynamics with microscopic particle behavior, introducing a probabilistic approach to understanding entropy.

Boltzmann's famous equation, \( S = k \ln W \), links the entropy of a system (\( S \)) to the number of microscopic configurations (\( W \)), with \( k \) being the Boltzmann constant. This equation is fundamental in explaining the second law of thermodynamics and the arrow of time.
