Discovery of Nuclear Fission

Overview

The **discovery of nuclear fission** occurred in 1938 when German scientists **Otto Hahn** and **Fritz Strassmann**, along with physicists **Lise Meitner** and **Otto Frisch**, discovered that the uranium atom could be split into smaller atoms when bombarded with neutrons. This groundbreaking discovery paved the way for nuclear energy and atomic weapons.

fission involves splitting an atomic nucleus into two smaller nuclei, releasing a tremendous amount of energy. The process also releases additional neutrons, which can trigger a chain reaction. This principle became the foundation for nuclear reactors and the development of atomic bombs.

Impact

The discovery of nuclear fission had profound scientific, political, and societal impacts. It led to the creation of nuclear reactors for energy production and the development of nuclear weapons during World War II, changing the course of history and shaping international relations for decades to come.
